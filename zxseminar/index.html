Monoidal categories

Theorem. Every monoidal category \(\mathcal C\) admits
a presentation of generators \(\mathcal G\) and relations \(\mathcal R\):

\[\mathcal C=\langle\mathcal G\mid\mathcal R\rangle\]

Joyal–Street (1991), Selinger (2011, §3.1)

Interpretation

\[\llbracket-\rrbracket:\langle\mathcal G\mid\mathcal R\rangle\longrightarrow\mathcal C\]

Defined inductively by:

\[\llbracket D_2\circ D_1\rrbracket=\llbracket D_2\rrbracket\circ\llbracket D_1\rrbracket\]
\[\llbracket D_2\otimes D_1\rrbracket=\llbracket D_2\rrbracket\otimes\llbracket D_1\rrbracket\]

It is enough to assign the generators and check the relations.

Properties of Interest

• Soundness
(well-defined)
\(\forall D_1,D_2,\quad\mathcal R\vdash D_1=D_2\Rightarrow\llbracket D_1\rrbracket=\llbracket D_2\rrbracket\)
• Universality
(full)
\(\forall M\in\mathcal C(n,m),\quad\exists D:n\to m\ \text{s.t.}\ \llbracket D\rrbracket=M\)
• Completeness
(faithful)
\(\forall D_1,D_2,\quad\llbracket D_1\rrbracket=\llbracket D_2\rrbracket\Rightarrow\mathcal R\vdash D_1=D_2\)
• Minimality
(axiom independence)
\(\forall R\in\mathcal R,\quad\mathcal R\setminus\{R\}\nvdash R\)

Bialgebra and Leibniz

Theorem. Let \(A\) be an algebra over a field \(k\).

A linear map \(\partial:A\to A\) satisfies the Leibniz rule if and only if the polynomial bialgebra \(k[t]\) acts on \(A\) compatibly with multiplication, with \(t\) acting as \(\partial\).

\[\Delta(t)=t\otimes1+1\otimes t\]
\[\boxed{\partial(DE)=(\partial D)E+D(\partial E)}\]

Kassel (1995), Lemma V.6.3

Derivatives in ZX

Definition. On formal finite sums of ZX diagrams with phases affine in \(\theta\), a diagrammatic derivative is a family

\[\partial_\theta:\mathbf{ZX}_{\theta}^{+}(n,m)\longrightarrow\mathbf{ZX}_{\theta}^{+}(n,m)\]

which preserves sums and satisfies both Leibniz rules:

\[\begin{aligned}\partial(D+E)&=\partial D+\partial E,\qquad \partial 0=0,\\[.45em]\partial(E\circ D)&=(\partial E)\circ D+E\circ(\partial D),\\[.45em]\partial(D\otimes E)&=(\partial D)\otimes E+D\otimes(\partial E).\end{aligned}\]

And it commutes with interpretation:

\[\boxed{\llbracket\partial_\theta D\rrbracket=\frac{\partial}{\partial\theta}\llbracket D\rrbracket}\]

Toumi, Yeung & de Felice (2021), §§2–3
